📜 Game Rules Summary

  1. FIBA 3x3 Official Rules (2021 edition) apply for all matches.
  2. Each game is 10 minutes long. First team to reach 21 points wins immediately.
  3. No timeouts and no stop-clock for Group Stage matches.
  4. For Bronze & Gold Medal games: clock stops in the last 1 minute, and each team is allowed one (1) timeout.
  5. If the score is tied at full time, a Golden Free Throw decides the winner.
